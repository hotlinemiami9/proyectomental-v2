# Pending legal and privacy work

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. The final texts must be reviewed by a qualified professional before production.

## Required before public launch

- [ ] Legal owner name and tax identifier.
- [ ] Registered address and contact details.
- [ ] Privacy policy in Spanish.
- [ ] Terms of service in Spanish.
- [ ] Cookie/tracking notice if the final analytics configuration uses cookies.
- [ ] Data processing agreements with Supabase, Cloudflare, SMTP provider, GlitchTip, and Umami hosting.
- [ ] Retention and deletion policy, including the 30-day backup window.
- [ ] Data subject rights procedure and response deadlines.
- [ ] International transfer assessment, if any provider processes outside the EU.
- [ ] Minors policy and guardian authorization; no age gate is currently planned.
- [ ] Human Design disclaimer clarifying that it is not science, medicine, psychology, or financial advice.
- [ ] Intellectual property and content licensing review for Human Design materials.
- [ ] App store privacy declarations before native app publication.
- [ ] Incident response and personal-data breach procedure.

- GDPR and Spain are the provisional legal framework.
